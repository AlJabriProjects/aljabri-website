Our Story

ALJABRI PROJECTS is a leading provider of industrial crushing and screening solutions based in Sohar, Sultanate of Oman. With over 25 years of industry experience, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for mining, construction, and industrial companies.

Our mission is to provide world-class crushing and screening equipment along with comprehensive support services. We are committed to delivering excellence in every project we undertake.

Founded on principles of quality, reliability, and innovation, ALJABRI PROJECTS has completed over 500 successful projects across the Middle East region.
